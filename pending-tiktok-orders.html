How these amounts are calculatedCards reflect the current filters. Shipping deadlines use the buyer’s earliest open due date. Item values exclude tax, and buyer-paid shipping is shown separately. Order amounts are shared across their original lines. When TikTok supplies an unsettled fee estimate, it takes priority. Otherwise we estimate the US standard 6% referral rate on payment plus platform discount less tax; qualifying categories can be 5%, and tiered high-value rates or affiliate fees can differ. An available complete TikTok statement replaces the estimate with fees and taxes charged by the platform. After-fee figures include buyer shipping and exclude postage and item costs.
