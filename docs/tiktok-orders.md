# TikTok order workflow

TikTok uses `pending-tiktok-orders.html` and the same page with `?view=history`. eBay continues using its existing pages, tables, RPCs, checkout and packaging. The only additions to eBay pages are links styled as marketplace tabs. There is no combined marketplace view.

The TikTok workspace reuses the eBay fulfillment styles and navigation shell, with expandable buyer cards, receipt links, shipping-label coverage, saved documents, preparation and notes. Shipping summaries and filters show overdue, today, tomorrow and missing dates. Date comparisons use calendar days in America/New_York (including daylight-saving changes); a date-only deadline becomes overdue the following day. Unknown deadlines never imply overdue status. A buyer's earliest verified deadline determines their group, and filters keep that buyer's orders together. Groups with any missing dates also appear under Date needed. Shipping dates on a buyer card opens the verification and editing flow. History excludes urgency indicators for completed orders.

## Export and import

1. In TikTok Manage orders, select Orders → All.
2. Export **All orders under current tab** as CSV to include cancelled orders as well as Awaiting shipment/collection. A Canceled-tab CSV can also refresh known cancellations.
3. In extension v1.0.21+, expand **OG · TikTok orders**, choose **Send orders to OG**, and select the downloaded CSV. Alternatively upload it directly in the TikTok workspace.
4. Review the count, item preview and actual export time, then confirm.
5. Export again after label purchases, package combinations and status changes. An All-orders export can refresh existing records into history, but does not backfill previously unseen historical/unpaid orders.

The parser preserves long IDs as text and rejects malformed rows, invalid quantities, conflicting duplicate records, unknown statuses and non-TikTok reports. Imports are transactional, serialized, audited and idempotent by file hash. Older snapshots cannot replace newer data. Missing orders/items never imply dispatch, cancellation or deletion. Reimports preserve staff notes and stages. Changed quantities on packed orders require review; dispatched orders retain their dispatch stage and receive an audit warning.

This initial workspace represents the OG Jewelers US shop. The CSV does not identify its originating shop, so staff must select that shop's export. Multi-shop importing requires an explicit shop identity before supporting additional accounts.

## Preparation and history

The TikTok-only tables are `tiktok_orders`, `tiktok_order_lines`, `tiktok_order_imports`, `tiktok_order_events` and `tiktok_order_documents`. Inventory staff can read through RLS; writes use permission-checked RPCs. Optimistic revisions prevent lost staff edits. Status changes and imports are recorded with the authenticated actor and server time.

OG stages are Pending, Preparing, Waiting, Packed, Dispatched and Completed. Waiting requires a reason. Packing requires physical-content confirmation. Dispatch requires Completed, tracking on every saved line and explicit carrier-handoff confirmation. Marketplace cancellations, unpaid status and return flags block packing/dispatch/completion. Canceled, shipped, completed or internally dispatched orders appear in TikTok history. **Complete order** requires saved label coverage, completion photos for the current item quantities, and a physical check; it moves the orders to TikTok history without claiming carrier handoff. Changed imported quantities reopen internally completed orders for review. Manual reopening uses **Revert order**, described below.

Preparation edits do not debit stock. Complete order atomically deducts the linked inventory quantities; it does not create sales, change TikTok or invoke eBay checkout. Staff can record a verified ship-by date, since TikTok's CSV omits deadlines. The extension can include deadlines from visible order rows; it does not claim to have traversed every page. Product descriptions are often generic, so matching the physical piece and the full TikTok receipt remains necessary.

### Manager corrections

Active managers and admins can expand a completed/dispatched order in history and choose **Revert order**. Enter a reason, sign with your own password, and confirm that returning the order to Pending and restoring stock is appropriate. The server checks the current employee role and order revision, restores only previously deducted inventory, and resets the inventory links to drafts. Repeated or stale submissions cannot restore stock twice. Reopened orders stay in Pending even if TikTok says Shipped; a new completion checks and deducts the linked inventory again.

The order's history records the authenticated manager, server time, reason, before/after order state, original items and inventory links, and stock correction transactions. Original photos, labels and certificates stay attached. Marketplace cancellations, returns and payment problems must be resolved before reopening. Ordinary preparation edits cannot reopen terminal work. This corrects Invsto fulfillment; it does not cancel, refund or change shipment status on TikTok. eBay history provides the equivalent manager action for an order group or individual line, preserving its existing inventory and bag safeguards and reversal audit.

## PDFs and videos

Watch receipt opens the full receipt in TikTok, under the staff member's TikTok login. Videos and signed media URLs are not copied or retained.

Extension **v1.0.17+** adds **Capture item photo → OG** beside the open TikTok receipt. Pause on the item and click Capture. The button shows the order number and SKU ID. For a multi-item order, the extension remembers the SKU of the receipt clicked; it refuses to guess when that identity is missing. Each OG item also has an **Open item video receipt** link that targets its SKU.

Only the visible video rectangle is cropped and sent to the configured TikTok workspace. OG saves the JPEG to the exact `(order_number, sku_id)` line. With extension **v1.0.18**, capture reuses the already-open TikTok workspace without reloading it. After saving, OG expands the buyer card, scrolls to the exact item, marks **Capture saved**, and returns focus to that tab. It opens no new tab or dialog. Queue filters are cleared only if they hide the saved item; completed orders appear in the separate TikTok history view. Keep the TikTok workspace open and signed in before capturing. Missing, outdated, or busy receivers prompt a retry instead of opening a new tab.

The private `tiktok-receipt-photos` bucket and `tiktok_order_receipt_photos` table are separate from eBay. Stills remain attached to stable line IDs through CSV refreshes and TikTok history. Saves are audited and idempotent by transfer ID. A missing match leaves a retry action; staff can import the order and retry within one hour. Successful saves clear the image from extension handoff storage. Direct handoffs are bound to the receiving OG tab, and the URL retains the transfer ID so reloading can recover an interrupted save. Raw videos, full-page screenshots, and signed TikTok media URLs are not retained.

Each capture has **Remove** beside its thumbnail and in the larger photo viewer. An inline **Remove capture / Cancel** confirmation removes only that capture from its order line. The browser deletes both stored JPEGs through the Storage API; the order and other captures stay intact. A small database record and audit event retain who removed it and prevent a delayed extension delivery from restoring it. Failed file deletion offers **Retry cleanup**; the next page load also resumes unfinished cleanup from the existing photo metadata without downloading the removed images. This control requires only an OG refresh, not an extension update.

Download an order packet for one order, one buyer or the filtered view (up to 250 orders). Packets are paginated 4×6 PDFs marked **ORDER PACKET - NOT POSTAGE**. Print order packet saves its PDF and uses the existing shipping-printer picker. Shipping labels are downloaded from TikTok and attached only after checking recipient/order coverage. Documents use private paths under `tiktok-orders/<user-id>/<sha256>.pdf` in the existing PDF storage bucket; associations and events exist only in TikTok tables. Printing does not change any order stage. Review the PDF before printing; historical attachments remain available even if TikTok replaces tracking later.

Export this view downloads a TikTok-only tracking CSV. It is not a TikTok source import format.

The extension prepares the larger JPEG (up to 1600 pixels on the long edge); OG creates a separate JPEG thumbnail (up to 320 pixels, quality 0.78) in the receiving browser before upload. Both immutable files must exist before the database attaches the capture and OG acknowledges it. Storage holds the files; the database holds their paths and byte sizes. There are no Supabase image transformations or image-processing Edge Functions. Uploads specify a one-hour browser cache lifetime. Only thumbnails near the viewport are requested; collapsed/offscreen cards do not fetch their photos. Clicking a thumbnail loads the larger photo in a dialog. Private signed URLs are batched (up to 100 paths), deduplicated while in flight, and reused for 25 minutes in the current page.

Older single-file captures get a browser-generated thumbnail on first visible use, without changing their order-line association. This requires a one-time download of the old larger image. Backfill failures leave the original viewable and are retried on the next page load. Concurrent staff backfills may leave an unused small object in the losing staff member’s folder; the first committed thumbnail is retained. Extension v1.0.17 already supplies the needed capture payload; this optimization does not require another extension release.

## Shipping labels and phone completion (extension 1.0.20)

1. Use **Create shipping label** on a single-order buyer, **Create combined label** for multiple items, or **Get shipping label(s)** when TikTok has already created the labels. Select buyers and use **Create / get shipping labels** for a batch. A selection includes every eligible order for that buyer even when search shows only one sibling. Orders awaiting shipment or collection are included when no label PDF is saved in OG, even if tracking already exists. Saved labels, cancellation/return holds, and completed/dispatched orders are excluded. Batches are limited to 50 orders.
2. The extension searches the exact string IDs in TikTok, switches to 50/page when needed, and verifies every row, live status and selection count. It opens **Arrange shipment** for new labels or **Print documents** for existing labels. If the imported status is stale, the live status decides the action. Mixed groups offer **Create new labels** and **Get existing labels** separately. Missing orders, AI search, blocked statuses or extra selections stop preparation. Success is reported only once TikTok's review panel is visible. Staff review package combinations, address, service, weight, dimensions, and postage in TikTok. The extension never confirms a combination or purchases postage.
3. Choose **Print → Print through OG**. The PDF opens the TikTok order workspace. OG matches order/package/tracking text locally; unmatched pages require staff input. A creation request can suggest a same-buyer single-page mapping, but never substitutes for PDF verification. Review each page and confirm coverage before **Save labels to orders**. Manual **Add shipping-label PDF** uses the same mapping flow.
4. OG splits PDFs locally and saves each order's matching pages. Orders sharing one package share the same file. Content-hash paths deduplicate uploads and the batch association is atomic and retry-safe. Standard A6 labels are fitted to 4×6 as vector PDFs in the browser. Choose the printer after saving; attachment does not automatically print. Successful attachment clears the extension PDF handoff. The handoff expires after one hour.
5. **Complete order → Use my phone**, or the buyer's **Use my phone**, opens a QR/link. Staff sign in on the phone and keep that camera page open. Send subsequent buyers to the same phone. It protects unsaved photos before switching orders. Saving photos updates the computer while its photo/pairing panel is open. Uploads are scoped to the exact current order IDs, SKUs and quantities; stale item sets must be refreshed.
6. **Complete order** opens a review of every order in that buyer's completion batch, including order IDs, quantities, receipt captures, buyer messages, preparation notes, and each order's label attachments. Missing labels and multiple labels are highlighted; each saved label has a preview. Take final order photos, choose files, or use the paired phone in the same review. **Refresh review** reloads saved orders and labels and clears the physical-confirmation checkbox; pending photos must be saved or removed first. Check the packed contents, labels, photos and inventory deductions, optionally add a completion note, confirm the checkbox, then **Complete order(s)**. Labels and photos remain available in separate TikTok history. Completion is all-or-nothing for the buyer batch, permission checked, revision checked, audited and retry-safe. It deducts only explicitly linked inventory quantities.

Deploy `20261018180000_tiktok_completion_review.sql` before publishing the review UI. `complete_tiktok_orders_review` uses the existing evidence, inventory and hold checks in the same transaction, then records the authenticated actor, note, selected order IDs, item quantities and label document IDs in each newly completed order's history. Notes are optional, limited to 4,000 characters, and do not overwrite preparation notes. A failed completion saves neither notes nor stock movements; a repeated successful request cannot duplicate the review audit. This review does not require an extension update.

Completion photos use private `tiktok-completion-photos` storage and `tiktok_order_completion_photos`, with separate authenticated phone-session/request tables. The browser creates JPEGs up to 1600px and thumbnails up to 320px before upload; no server image transformation runs. Partial upload retries reuse paths. Signed previews are cached; only open photo/pairing panels poll, every 12 seconds while visible, and phone heartbeats write at most every 30 seconds. Photo removal/replacement preserves audit evidence; removed completion originals remain private for that audit. Receipt-capture removal separately deletes its two files as described above.

Additional checks: `tests/tiktok-shipping.test.mjs`, `tests/tiktok-fulfillment-database.test.mjs`, `tests/tiktok-fulfillment-browser.test.mjs`, and `tests/tiktok-online-print.test.mjs` cover exact native selection, protected handoffs, page splitting, upload retries, authenticated phone pairing, completion guards and marketplace isolation.

## Inventory and item preparation

Each item has **Scan / link inventory**. Select the store, scan a barcode (including leading zeroes), choose the exact inventory item and source, and check the quantity. Linking is a draft, not a stock reservation or deduction. Existing shared reservations remain protected. Complete order rechecks stock and deducts the still-undeducted linked quantity in the same transaction as photos, labels, revision checks and completion. A failed check rolls everything back. Retrying or reopening an already deducted line cannot deduct the same units twice. Unlinked items do not change inventory. TikTok stock movements identify the exact order, line, source and staff member; no eBay completion function is invoked.

**Item found / Item not found**, **Watch**, **Watch missing / Mark watch found**, and **Set Pending CGL** are separate, persistent item records. A missing watch requires physical confirmation when found. Finding it does not release its CGL requirement. Removing a CGL requirement needs a correction reason; removing Watch cannot silently discard either requirement. Closed order controls are read-only. All work filters keep buyer bundles together; Ready, Waiting, Item not found, Watch missing, Pending CGL and Pending cancellation have separate views and badges. An explicit not-found, missing-watch, pending CGL, or cancellation hold blocks completion for that buyer. This TikTok flow completes whole orders; it does not yet split a partially fulfilled order like eBay's partial-shipment flow.

**CGL certificate** accepts QR/link, PDF and photos, report number and watch serial. Supported CGL QR links archive the PDF on demand through `archive-tiktok-certificate`, reusing the existing validated provider-download helper without changing the eBay function. Uploading a copy alone does not clear Pending CGL: staff must explicitly confirm it belongs to this watch. Copies use a separate private `tiktok-order-certificates` bucket and stable TikTok line IDs. An author/admin may mark a copy incorrect with a reason; originals remain for audit and the confirmed requirement reopens. Copies load only on request and involve no image transformations.

**Cancellation hold** records a reason and holds the buyer's OG work. It links to TikTok for the actual cancellation; it does not claim to cancel the sale or issue a refund. Import a fresh TikTok export to confirm the marketplace result. **Manage cancellation hold** can update the reason or release a mistaken/reversed hold with an audited reason. A cancelled source order goes to TikTok history without consuming inventory.

The tracking CSV includes found/watch/CGL/cancellation markers and linked/deducted inventory quantities. The new line-work/cancellation/certificate tables are TikTok-only. Completion and stage guards run on the server even if a second staff screen is stale. Certificate copies are never mixed with eBay records.

A shipped/completed TikTok import with undeducted inventory links remains in the OG pending queue for completion review. Importing a marketplace status never silently checks out stock. After OG completion, carrier-handoff confirmation can move Completed directly to Dispatched without reopening the order.


## Purchase dates and confirmed cancellations (extension 1.0.21)

**Purchase date** matches the Created Time calendar date from TikTok's CSV. A match on any order keeps all of that buyer's orders and item lines in the current Pending/History view, including other purchase dates. It never selects an arbitrary timezone for source dates or splits a buyer bundle. Clear date restores the unfiltered queue.

**Mark cancelled** records an already confirmed TikTok cancellation for the exact order. Staff must verify the order on TikTok, enter a reason and check the confirmation. The order moves to TikTok history; its buyer's other pending orders remain. Server-side permission and revision checks, authenticated staff identity, timestamp, reason and complete before/after states are recorded. It releases that order's pending cancellation hold but never refunds, deducts/restores inventory, removes captures or deletes fulfillment evidence. Managers/admins can correct a mistaken manual mark with a reason; its original status and pending cancellation hold are restored. Export-confirmed cancellations cannot be manually reopened.

**Update TikTok statuses / cancellations** opens the CSV instructions. The extension's **All orders (include cancellations)** button selects TikTok's All tab; export the whole tab as CSV, then Send orders to OG and review the cancellation count and statuses. Existing orders are refreshed, while unseen historical orders are skipped. Missing rows never imply cancellation. The normal Refresh button only reloads OG's saved data. No automatic TikTok API sync is implied.

Deploy `20261018220000_tiktok_cancellation_sync.sql` before these assets. Its import trigger logs cancellation/status changes with source import and staff identity. Older reports are ignored by the existing importer. A newer report contradicting an already recorded cancellation retains the cancellation and logs a conflict for manager review; it cannot silently return the order to shipping. The download ZIP/release manifest are extension 1.0.21 with unchanged permissions. Extract over the installed extension folder, reload the extension, then refresh TikTok and OG.
