# Weekly eBay seller, day and show breakdown

In Dashboard → Weekly sales, each purchase week and the month total now have **By seller, day & show**. The expansion offers seller totals, purchase days (Eastern), and shows. Each group includes original item sales, cancellations, refunds, amounts under review and verified net item sales. Order buttons filter the existing order browser; a day/show or seller/show button retains that narrower scope. Show links open the eBay replay.

`weekly_ebay_production` receives the same classified orders as `get_weekly_sales_for_market`. It assigns each order to one bucket, preserving all five monetary totals exactly. It does not recompute sale values from auction wins or add captured attempts to API orders. An order with multiple known shows stays in a visible review bucket; order-level refunds are not arbitrarily allocated between shows.

Seller precedence is: explicit report override, unanimous recorded line/attempt seller, single recorded show seller, then the user's Eastern-time rule. The Eastern-time fallback is Otello for starts at 18:00–07:59. At 08:00–17:59, Saturday/Sunday shows default to Manuella and Monday–Friday shows default to Sydney. The show start day controls the rule, even when orders cross midnight. Without a show, a precise purchase timestamp supplies the fallback. Date-only orders stay unassigned. Multiple recorded sellers stay in review. These defaults affect reporting, not payroll, commissions, inventory, or order ownership.

Confirmed show links use non-merged, non-excluded live attempts linked to exact order lines. Where capture is incomplete, a unique scheduled show can supply an **estimated** link: purchase time falls between its start and the next show or eight hours later, whichever is earlier. A verified end shortens the interval. Simultaneous shows do not establish a unique link. Off-show purchases may fall inside those estimates, which are visibly labeled. The method disclosure describes the rule; unmatched sales remain visible and included in totals. A show crossing midnight appears under each purchase day; the show view combines its orders within the selected week/month.

The private `weekly_ebay_show_catalog` supplements recorded sessions with observed Live Studio event metadata. Future captured sessions are read automatically. **Assign seller** saves an admin-only report override, with an audit row and optimistic revision check. Clearing it restores recorded-seller/time-rule behavior. Existing operational seller assignments are preserved.

Validation: database coverage for ET boundaries, DST, midnight, unknown precision, ambiguous shows/sellers, unchanged totals, access controls and audited overrides; desktop/phone browser coverage for all three views, zero-sale days, refresh preservation, filtered orders, corrections and save failures. Production verification rolls back its entire transaction and compares every monetary subtotal to the existing weekly report.

## Printable reports and streamer hours

Each expansion offers **Generate report** and **Streamer hours summary**. Both open a frozen snapshot for the selected week or month, with Print / Save PDF and Download CSV. Dates include weekdays. Show schedules are visually grouped into separate daily tables with full weekday/date captions, show counts and consistent columns, ordered by Eastern scheduled start. Undated shows and sales without a single show link have separate sections. Daily blocks stay together when printing where they fit on one page. The report includes seller and daily sales, show schedules and reported lengths, streaming hours by streamer, and attribution coverage. The hours requested here are streamer airtime, not employee timeclock hours; no timeclock records are queried.

The private timing helper supplements the report with all known shows starting in the month, including zero-sale shows, plus the preceding eight hours for overnight context. Airtime is counted once per distinct event, only for shows whose recorded start falls inside the selected dates. Sales still follow purchase dates, so a show can have period sales without its full airtime counting in that period. Missing durations are excluded from averages and labeled; simultaneous/mixed seller assignments put the show's airtime into one review group, not both sellers' totals. Linked orders without timing metadata retain a show row and their money.

`airtime_seconds`, source, checked timestamp and provisional status are independent of `ends_at`. Live Studio's **Length of stream** measures reported airtime. Event-list and captured starts are schedule evidence, not verified actual go-live times. The report displays both when they differ; it never fabricates an actual end by adding duration to the schedule. Saved start discrepancies do not silently change existing order attribution.

On November 8, all 17 visible Insights pages were checked: 143,494 seconds (39h 51m 34s) total; two pages still said Insights processing. After the user’s weekend correction, report attribution gives Otello 6 shows / 11h 39m 37s, Sydney 10 shows / 26h 41m 57s, and Manuella 1 show / 1h 30m. The Saturday November 7 show at 5:30 PM Eastern moves from Sydney to Manuella; its 3:40 AM show stays with Otello. These values are explicitly a saved snapshot, not a claim that future show durations sync automatically. New shows without saved timing display Not recorded.

Validation covers deduplicated airtime, cross-week shows, zero-sale and untimed shows, private timing access, mobile/desktop report generation, PDF print isolation, CSV downloads and formula escaping. Printed pages are rendered and visually checked before release.

## Historical streamer analysis

The report ends with **Historical streamer analysis** and defaults to **All available**, with optional 3/6/12-month ranges. Its admin-only `get_streamer_performance_history` RPC reuses the weekly merchandise/reversal rules and exact order-line show links. It returns show-level amounts and provenance without customer details. Loading, retry and failure states remain visible; print/CSV wait for the requested history to finish. The analysis appears in both exports.

Each streamer has a selected-period versus prior same-weekday comparison, a month/weekday matrix, early/middle/late-month results, and two-hour Eastern start windows. Comparisons use medians, means, interquartile ranges, sales per hour and sample sizes. Historical baselines strictly precede the selected report. Weekday totals combine a streamer's shows starting on the same Eastern date. An overnight show belongs once to its start date with full linked sales and airtime, even across a month boundary; the query includes the next purchase day. A private clone of the existing matcher extends an estimated window past eight hours when saved airtime is longer, still capped at the next show or a verified end. It does not fabricate actual end timestamps or modify the weekly purchase-date logic.

In-progress dates, processing insights, unknown/mixed hosts, unavailable completion evidence and materially incomplete order coverage are excluded. If any known show for a streamer/date is excluded, that whole date is omitted from daily comparisons. Missing shows are not zero days. Zero-sales shows need verified zero paid items and completion. A coverage check excludes shows whose linked lines are below 80% of eBay's paid-item count; the report explains that these counts are not identical measures. Hourly comparisons require at least 15 minutes of finalized airtime. Exclusions and individual show evidence appear in CSV.

Start-window suggestions compare the same streamer, weekday, calendar month and title-based show type. At least five timed shows across two months, three matched groups and five shows on each side are required. The displayed change is the median of within-group median-rate ratios; it is not a significance test or causal claim. Other windows display limited evidence. Product titles only approximate inventory mix. The report recommends testing comparable inventory before changing the schedule.

The historical host fallback retains Otello overnight. Before September 15, 2026, unrecorded daytime hosts remain unassigned; that cutoff approximates the user's mid-September estimate for Sydney. From that date, weekday daytime defaults to Sydney and weekend daytime to Manuella. Explicit/recorded hosts still win, including any earlier confirmed Sydney assignment. This affects analytical attribution, never employee timeclock records or commissions.

November 8 history reconciliation read the visible eBay Live Studio event list and Insights pages: 156 published replays across June–November, including 139 earlier replays. It recovered 138 older airtimes; one June show reports Data unavailable, and a draft event was excluded. These are saved source observations, not an automatic promise that future eBay Insights will refresh. eBay's gross sales metric includes tax/shipping and is stored only as evidence; it is never mixed with reconciled net merchandise sales.

Validation includes admin access, range limits, reversals, overnight and month-boundary sales beyond eight hours, DST, no baseline leakage, incomplete-day exclusion, robust medians, matched-sample thresholds, escaping, retry/range selection, mobile/desktop layout, CSV and PDF print isolation.

### Full historical backfill

The expanded November 8 reconciliation observed all published replays returned by the date-filtered Live Studio list. The January 2020–June 2025 range returned no events. July–September 2025 returned 16 published replays and one excluded draft; the first published event was August 7, 2025 (titled “JOIN OUR FIRST SHOW”). October–December returned 47; January–March 2026 returned 54; April–May returned 40. Each range was below the list's 100-row display limit. Combined with the prior June–November collection, the catalog contains 313 published shows, 312 reported durations, and 309 reported sales metrics. Four shows have unavailable sales; two recent shows retain their processing flag. Missing values remain null rather than becoming zero sales.

`tools/prepare-ebay-show-history.mjs events.json insights.json output.sql` creates a reviewable SQL import from saved visible-page observations. It rejects missing Insights observations, ignores drafts, deduplicates event IDs, handles explicit EDT/EST offsets, and preserves existing seller overrides and verified ends. The private Insights JSON retains original metric labels/values, source URL, observation time, audience, buyers, paid items, conversion and engagement evidence. Selected source metrics are also included in the report CSV, clearly separate from net merchandise sales. No order, inventory, commission or timeclock records are changed.

All-history queries can resolve the earliest saved show with a null start. The dashboard first requests the latest three calendar months and uses the returned earliest-show date to load adjacent earlier ranges of at most three months. It combines distinct shows only after every request succeeds. Superseded requests stop loading older ranges; errors never export a partial result. Coverage counts use the requested purchase dates, excluding the extra overnight buffer, so batches add without double-counting. Show-level totals still include their overnight purchases. Explicit API ranges retain the 370-day bound and every request retains the active-admin gate.

Validation adds old-date coverage, adjacent-range reconciliation, loading cancellation/failure, source-metric export, missing-versus-zero values, and a rollback comparison showing unchanged current-week order amounts and financial totals.
