# Weekly eBay seller, day and show breakdown

In Dashboard → Weekly sales, each purchase week and the month total now have **By seller, day & show**. The expansion offers seller totals, purchase days (Eastern), and shows. Each group includes original item sales, cancellations, refunds, amounts under review and verified net item sales. Order buttons filter the existing order browser; a day/show or seller/show button retains that narrower scope. Show links open the eBay replay.

`weekly_ebay_production` receives the same classified orders as `get_weekly_sales_for_market`. It assigns each order to one bucket, preserving all five monetary totals exactly. It does not recompute sale values from auction wins or add captured attempts to API orders. An order with multiple known shows stays in a visible review bucket; order-level refunds are not arbitrarily allocated between shows.

Seller precedence is: explicit report override, unanimous recorded line/attempt seller, single recorded show seller, then the user's Eastern-time rule. The Eastern-time fallback is Otello for starts at 18:00–07:59. At 08:00–17:59, Saturday/Sunday shows default to Manuella and Monday–Friday shows default to Sydney. The show start day controls the rule, even when orders cross midnight. Without a show, a precise purchase timestamp supplies the fallback. Date-only orders stay unassigned. Multiple recorded sellers stay in review. These defaults affect reporting, not payroll, commissions, inventory, or order ownership.

Confirmed show links use non-merged, non-excluded live attempts linked to exact order lines. Where capture is incomplete, a unique scheduled show can supply an **estimated** link: purchase time falls between its start and the next show or eight hours later, whichever is earlier. A verified end shortens the interval. Simultaneous shows do not establish a unique link. Off-show purchases may fall inside those estimates, which are visibly labeled. The method disclosure describes the rule; unmatched sales remain visible and included in totals. A show crossing midnight appears under each purchase day; the show view combines its orders within the selected week/month.

The private `weekly_ebay_show_catalog` supplements recorded sessions with observed Live Studio event metadata. Future captured sessions are read automatically. **Assign seller** saves an admin-only report override, with an audit row and optimistic revision check. Clearing it restores recorded-seller/time-rule behavior. Existing operational seller assignments are preserved.

Validation: database coverage for ET boundaries, DST, midnight, unknown precision, ambiguous shows/sellers, unchanged totals, access controls and audited overrides; desktop/phone browser coverage for all three views, zero-sale days, refresh preservation, filtered orders, corrections and save failures. Production verification rolls back its entire transaction and compares every monetary subtotal to the existing weekly report.

November 8 reconciliation: Live Studio's visible event list supplied 17 event IDs/titles/start times for November 2–7. Eight already had capture connections; nine lacked connections. Browser access stopped before missing Activity/Sold history could be replayed, so the catalog import is schedule evidence, not a completed sales-history recovery. At verification, 189 orders had exact capture links, 320 had estimated show-time links and one remained unmatched. All 510 orders still reconciled to the existing $60,835.48 net total.

## Printable reports and streamer hours

Each expansion offers **Generate report** and **Streamer hours summary**. Both open a frozen snapshot for the selected week or month, with Print / Save PDF and Download CSV. Dates include weekdays. Show schedules are visually grouped into separate daily tables with full weekday/date captions, show counts and consistent columns, ordered by Eastern scheduled start. Undated shows and sales without a single show link have separate sections. Daily blocks stay together when printing where they fit on one page. The report includes seller and daily sales, show schedules and reported lengths, streaming hours by streamer, and attribution coverage. The hours requested here are streamer airtime, not employee timeclock hours; no timeclock records are queried.

The private timing helper supplements the report with all known shows starting in the month, including zero-sale shows, plus the preceding eight hours for overnight context. Airtime is counted once per distinct event, only for shows whose recorded start falls inside the selected dates. Sales still follow purchase dates, so a show can have period sales without its full airtime counting in that period. Missing durations are excluded from averages and labeled; simultaneous/mixed seller assignments put the show's airtime into one review group, not both sellers' totals. Linked orders without timing metadata retain a show row and their money.

`airtime_seconds`, source, checked timestamp and provisional status are independent of `ends_at`. Live Studio's **Length of stream** measures reported airtime. Event-list and captured starts are schedule evidence, not verified actual go-live times. The report displays both when they differ; it never fabricates an actual end by adding duration to the schedule. Saved start discrepancies do not silently change existing order attribution.

On November 8, all 17 visible Insights pages were checked: 143,494 seconds (39h 51m 34s) total; two pages still said Insights processing. After the user’s weekend correction, report attribution gives Otello 6 shows / 11h 39m 37s, Sydney 10 shows / 26h 41m 57s, and Manuella 1 show / 1h 30m. The Saturday November 7 show at 5:30 PM Eastern moves from Sydney to Manuella; its 3:40 AM show stays with Otello. These values are explicitly a saved snapshot, not a claim that future show durations sync automatically. New shows without saved timing display Not recorded.

Validation covers deduplicated airtime, cross-week shows, zero-sale and untimed shows, private timing access, mobile/desktop report generation, PDF print isolation, CSV downloads and formula escaping. Printed pages are rendered and visually checked before release.
