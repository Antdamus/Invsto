Our Heritage

Jewelry is not just a business for us — it is a family tradition.

Founder Otello Guillen, proud of his Venezuelan heritage, grew up surrounded by the world of gemstones, gold, and craftsmanship. From a young age he learned the fundamentals of the trade: how to evaluate stones, how precious metals behave, and how true quality reveals itself through experience.

What began generations ago as a family trade evolved into a deep expertise in sourcing, evaluating, and delivering fine jewelry with integrity.

Today, OG Jewelers carries that legacy forward — blending old-world knowledge with modern access to the global jewelry market.

Our philosophy remains simple:
knowledge first, quality always.

We Are the Source

Most jewelry stores sit at the end of a long supply chain.

  • Manufacturers.
  • Wholesalers.
  • Distributors.
  • Retailers.

Each layer adds cost.

At OG Jewelers, we remove the middleman.

Because of our industry relationships and direct sourcing experience, we operate closer to the origin of the jewelry market. This allows us to offer pieces that combine exceptional craftsmanship with fair value.

Our clients benefit from:

  • Direct access to premium stones and metals
  • Transparent pricing
  • Honest expertise built on real industry experience

When we say "We Are the Source", it is not a slogan — it is how we operate.

The Miami Diamond District

OG Jewelers is located in the heart of Miami, Florida, one of the most dynamic jewelry markets in the United States.

The Miami Diamond District is a place where global trade, craftsmanship, and culture intersect. Jewelers, dealers, and collectors from around the world pass through this market every day.

Operating in this environment gives us constant exposure to:

  • international jewelry trends
  • competitive sourcing opportunities
  • rare and unique pieces

This global energy is part of what allows OG Jewelers to deliver exceptional pieces to our clients.
