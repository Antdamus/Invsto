A note from Otello

Built on family, craft, and the relationships behind every piece.

What we build begins with where we come from.

Otello Guillen, founder of OG Jewelers, comes from a family where jewelry is more than a business. It is part of their story. Originally from Venezuela, he is a fourth-generation jeweler who grew up surrounded by precious metals, gemstones, and the art of serving people through beautiful pieces.

His love for the trade started early. As a child, he would stand outside his father's jewelry business and try to sell little "precious stones" he had gathered from the local playground to passing customers. Even then, the spark was there: a natural eye for beauty, a love for people, and the instinct to build something of his own.

Today, that same passion lives at the heart of OG Jewelers. As a GIA gemologist specialist, Otello brings both deep expertise and genuine care to every piece and every client interaction. When you shop with OG, you are not buying through layers of middlemen or inflated retail markups. You are buying from people who know the craft, respect the materials, and value the relationship behind every purchase.

Only the best. Nothing less.
