How to read this report

Confirmed paid totals exclude pending, failed, canceled, resolved and payment-held purchases. A purchase count is an auction/item line; a bundle can contain more physical pieces. Missing costs are never treated as zero. Gross margin is paid item revenue minus base cost, before expenses and refunds. Estimated profit uses the optional fee and shipping assumptions. This is a live estimate, not a final eBay settlement or refund accounting report.

The on-screen report refreshes every five seconds while open. New order numbers and saved video links appear after they are imported and matched; no exact availability time is assumed. A PDF is a dated snapshot. Order links and images may require sign-in.
